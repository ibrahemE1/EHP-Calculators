

EHP - Engineering For Hydraulics & Pneumatics

EHP Calculators is a specialized engineering platform developed to be the primary digital reference for engineers, technicians, and engineering students working in the fields of hydraulics and pneumatics in Egypt and the Arab world.

We understand that daily hydraulic calculations — from calculating pump power, cylinder thrust force, flow rate, pressure loss, to pipe and tank sizing — are time-consuming and require high accuracy, where a small error can cost a lot in design or maintenance. Therefore, this platform was created to transform complex equations into smart, fast, and accurate calculators.

What Does The Platform Offer?

The platform provides a comprehensive set of practical engineering calculators, including:

Hydraulic Power Calculations: Calculating the power of hydraulic pumps and motors based on pressure and flow.
Cylinder Calculations: Calculating force, speed, stroke time, and required oil volume.
Flow & Pressure Calculations: Unit conversions, calculating fluid velocity inside pipes, and pressure drop.
Pneumatic Calculations: Air consumption calculations, pneumatic cylinder force, and valve sizing.
Auxiliary Design Calculations: Hose diameter selection, reservoir calculations, and safety factors.
Who Is This Tool For?

For Design & Maintenance Engineers: To make a fast and accurate decision on-site without needing to refer to catalogs.
For Professional Technicians: To simplify complex calculations and reduce the margin of error.
For Engineering Students: As an educational tool that bridges theory and practice and helps them understand and apply laws.
